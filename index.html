What is Honkestra?

A web app that turns nearby cars into a horn orchestra. Each car contributes its own pitch — together, you play real songs on real streets.

📍

Finds drivers near you

Uses GPS to discover other Honkestra users within a few hundred metres — or use a 4-letter friend code for private events.

🎵

50+ songs, growing

Happy Birthday, Imperial March, We Will Rock You, Mexican Hat Dance, Auld Lang Syne, and more. Or create your own with the rhythm maker.

🛣️

Two modes

Game mode — practice on your phone alone. Real World mode — phone stays silent and just tells you when to honk your actual car horn.

📹

Records as TikTok video

Capture the moment on camera. The app auto-records and gives you a vertical clip ready to share.

🥊

Honk Battles

Split the convoy into Red vs Blue teams. Whoever hits more cues wins. Great for parties and tailgates.

🚗

80+ cars to pick from

Every car has its real-world horn pitch — Mercedes deep, Ferrari bright, Mack truck rumble. Record your own real horn too.

How it works

Four taps, you're jamming.

1

Pick your car

Browse 80+ makes & models — each has its real factory horn pitch.

2

Find a room

Auto-discover drivers nearby, or share a 4-letter code with your group.

3

Pick a song

Everyone in the room hits Ready — host starts a 6-second countdown.

4

Honk on cue

Phone shows when, your horn makes the sound. Together you make music.

📱 Install on your phone

Open Honkestra once, then add it to your home screen so it launches like a real app.

  1. iPhone: Open in Safari → tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
  2. Android: Open in Chrome → tap ⋮ → "Add to Home Screen"
  3. The app icon appears next to your other apps. Tap to launch fullscreen.
