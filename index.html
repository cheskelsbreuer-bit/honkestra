How does this find other drivers?

Your phone connects to a tiny cloud relay (Supabase Realtime — encrypted, no signup needed). It broadcasts your GPS spot and car type and listens for other phones in the same area. We never store your location anywhere. Or skip GPS and use a 4-digit friend code with people you know.
